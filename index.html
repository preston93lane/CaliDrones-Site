What we fly

Four kinds of work, one standard: the right aircraft for the job, a flight plan cleared for the airspace, and files you can use the day they arrive.

Cell tower inspections

Close-up, high-resolution imagery of antennas, mounts, cabling and steel without sending a climber up first. We document every sector and elevation so your engineers can spot problems from the ground.

  • Sector-by-sector photo sets
  • Antenna and mount close-ups
  • Orbit and plumb video
  • Organized, labeled files

3D modeling & photogrammetry

Hundreds of overlapping photos processed into measurable 3D models, orthomosaic maps and point clouds. Useful for site planning, progress tracking, volume calculations and asset records.

  • Textured 3D models
  • Orthomosaic maps
  • Point clouds
  • Measurements and volumes

Real estate photo & video

Residential and commercial listings shown from the angles that sell them: the lot, the neighborhood, the view and the drive in. Edited stills and video, ready for MLS and social.

  • Edited aerial stills
  • Listing and walkthrough video
  • Twilight shoots
  • Vertical cuts for social

Cinematic aerial & FPV

Sweeping establishing shots from stabilized camera drones, and fast, close, flowing FPV runs through buildings, venues and landscapes. Shot for brands, events, film and tourism.

  • 4K stabilized aerials
  • FPV fly-throughs
  • Color-graded footage
  • Edited reels on request

How a project works

  1. Tell us about the project

    Send the location, what you need captured and when. Photos, plans or reference shots help.

  2. Get a custom quote

    Every site is different, so we price each job individually. You get a clear scope and price before anything is booked.

  3. We plan and fly

    We check airspace, secure FAA authorization where it's required, review the weather, and fly the mission safely.

  4. You get your files

    Edited imagery, video or models delivered through a download link, organized and named so they're easy to use.
