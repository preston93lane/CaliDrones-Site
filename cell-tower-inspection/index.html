Questions

Can a drone fly above 400 feet to inspect a tall tower?

Yes, within limits. FAA Part 107 lets a drone fly higher than 400 feet above ground when it stays within a 400-foot radius of a structure and no more than 400 feet above the structure's top. That covers inspection of most towers.

Do you work directly for carriers?

Our 2,145 towers were inspected on AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon sites. We work for the contractors, engineering firms, tower owners and platforms that deliver that work, and we follow the checklist you give us.

What do the deliverables look like?

Sector-by-sector photo sets, antenna and mount close-ups, orbit and plumb video, and compound views, organized and labeled by site, sector and view. Tell us your naming convention and we'll use it.

Do you cover sites outside California?

Yes. We're based in Los Angeles and cover all of California, and we've flown projects in 26 states and territories, including Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Travel is quoted with the job.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We're fully insured and can issue a certificate of insurance for your vendor file, including additional-insured wording when your contract requires it.

How is a tower inspection priced?

Per site, based on the deliverable checklist, the number of sites, travel and airspace. Send the list and you'll get a clear per-site price before anything is booked.
