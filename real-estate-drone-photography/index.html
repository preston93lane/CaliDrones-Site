Aerials answer the questions buyers ask first: how big is the lot, what's next door, how far is the beach or the trailhead, and what does the approach look like. Stills are edited for natural color and level horizons. Video is cut to music for the listing and in vertical format for Instagram and TikTok.

Twilight shoots are flown in the short window after sunset, with the anti-collision lighting the FAA requires, when the house lights and the sky look their best.

Land and lots

For vacant land, rebuild parcels and large estates, aerials do most of the selling. We can show the lot in its setting and add simple boundary or feature callouts when you supply the information.

Questions

Does a drone photographer need a license?

Yes. Anyone flying a drone for paid work needs an FAA Part 107 remote pilot certificate. We're Part 107 certified and fully insured.

Can you fly near LAX, Burbank or Van Nuys?

Often, yes. Much of Los Angeles is in controlled airspace. We request LAANC authorization before the shoot, and further FAA coordination where it's required. A few spots can't be flown, and we'll tell you up front.

Do you shoot twilight?

Yes. Twilight aerials are flown just after sunset with anti-collision lighting, as FAA rules require.

Do you shoot interiors?

We focus on aerials and aerial video, and work alongside your interior photographer. Photography companies can add our aerials to their own packages.

What if the weather's bad?

We watch the forecast and reschedule for wind, rain or low cloud at no charge, so your listing photos look right.

Do you cover Ventura County?

Yes. We shoot listings across Los Angeles and Ventura County, including Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Simi Valley, Camarillo, Oxnard and Ventura.
