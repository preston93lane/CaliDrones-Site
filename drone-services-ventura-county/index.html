Flying in Ventura County: airspace and terrain

Airports and the base. Oxnard and Camarillo airports have controlled airspace that we authorize through LAANC before the flight. Naval Base Ventura County at Point Mugu has its own controlled airspace and military operating areas over the coast. Flights near the base need careful planning, and some can't be done. We check before quoting.

Coast and canyons. Marine layer and afternoon wind shape the schedule on the coast. Inland, canyons and hillsides call for planned launch points and line of sight. We plan around both so the footage is clean.

Agriculture and open land. The Oxnard Plain and the Santa Clara River Valley are some of California's most productive farmland. Mapping flights over fields, orchards and packing operations give owners a measurable record of their land and facilities.

Parks. Drone take-offs and landings are prohibited on National Park Service land, including NPS-managed areas of the Santa Monica Mountains, and restricted in many state and county parks. We plan launch points accordingly.

Questions

Do you serve all of Ventura County?

Yes, from the coast to Ojai and the Santa Clara River Valley, and from Simi Valley to Thousand Oaks and Westlake Village.

Can you fly near Point Mugu?

Naval Base Ventura County at Point Mugu has its own controlled and military airspace. Some nearby locations can be authorized and some can't. We check the exact spot before quoting.

Can you map farmland?

Yes. We map fields, orchards and facilities into orthomosaics and 3D models for planning and records.

Do you shoot real estate in Ventura County?

Yes. We shoot aerials for homes, ranches, estates and land across the county, including twilight and vertical social cuts.

Are you insured?

Yes. Every flight is covered by liability insurance, and certificates of insurance are available on request.
