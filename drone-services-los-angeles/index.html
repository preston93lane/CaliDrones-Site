Flying in Los Angeles: airspace, permits and parks

Controlled airspace is everywhere. LAX sits under Class B airspace, Burbank is Class C, and Van Nuys, Long Beach, Santa Monica, Hawthorne and others have their own controlled zones. Much of the city needs authorization before a drone can fly. We request LAANC authorization ahead of the flight. For grids where LAANC can't approve the job, we coordinate with the FAA directly, and we'll tell you up front if a spot can't be flown.

Stadium and event restrictions. Temporary flight restrictions cover major stadium events at venues like SoFi Stadium, Dodger Stadium and the Rose Bowl. Shoots nearby are scheduled around them.

Filming permits. Commercial filming on public property in the City of Los Angeles and much of the county goes through FilmLA. We can fly under your production's permit and provide the pilot certificate and insurance documents it requires.

Parks and mountains. Drone take-offs and landings are prohibited on National Park Service land, including NPS-managed areas of the Santa Monica Mountains, and restricted in many state and local parks. We plan launch points on private land or other permitted sites.

Questions

Can you fly near LAX?

Often, yes, with authorization. Most LA airspace near airports can be authorized through LAANC before the flight. Some grids need further FAA coordination, and a few locations can't be flown. We check before quoting.

Do you need a permit to film with a drone in Los Angeles?

Commercial filming on public property in the City of Los Angeles and much of LA County requires a FilmLA permit. Private property requires the owner's permission. We can fly under your production's permit.

Can you fly in the Santa Monica Mountains?

Drones can't take off or land on National Park Service land there, and many state and local parks restrict them. We plan launch points on private land or other permitted sites when the job allows.

Are you insured?

Yes. Every flight is covered by liability insurance, and we can provide a certificate of insurance for your records.

How quickly can you fly?

It depends on the airspace, the weather and the scope. Many jobs can be scheduled within days. Send the location and timeline and you'll get a straight answer.
