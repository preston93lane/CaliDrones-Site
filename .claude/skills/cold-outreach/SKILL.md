# Cold outreach

## Principles
- One prospect, one reason. Open with the sourced hook, not with the company. Make one ask that is easy to say yes to.
- Short: first email under 120 words, follow-ups under 70. Plain text, no images, at most one link (the relevant service page).
- Proof only from `.claude/business-facts.md`. The strongest proof by segment:
  - Telecom: 2,145 towers inspected on AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon sites; 26 states and territories; Mavic 3 Enterprise.
  - Construction / mapping: same inspection discipline; orthomosaics, 3D models, volumes; LAANC handled.
  - Real estate: Part 107, insured, LAANC near LAX/BUR/VNY; twilight and vertical cuts.
  - FPV: Raging Waters FPV.
- Low-friction asks: "Worth a 10-minute call?", "Can I get on your vendor list?", "Want a sample flight on your next site?" Offer a paid pilot job at a fair intro price only if the owner has approved that offer.

## Sequence (business days)
- Day 0: email 1 (hook + proof + ask)
- Day 3: follow-up 1 (new angle or a deliverable example)
- Day 8: follow-up 2 (short close-the-loop)
- Optional on day 1: LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters, no pitch). Optional on day 4: phone call to the main line for vendor-onboarding contacts.
- Stop at the first reply, a bounce or an opt-out. Log every touch in the Lead Tracker.

## Volume and deliverability
- Hand-personalized, 15 to 30 new prospects a day maximum from the owner's real mailbox. Don't use the main domain for bulk sends; if volume ever grows past ~50 a day, use a separate sending domain with warm-up.
- No tracking pixels, no link shorteners.

## Compliance (CAN-SPAM; this is not legal advice)
- Honest From name and subject. Identify the sender and business.
- Include a valid postal address (a PO box works) and a plain opt-out line ("Reply 'no thanks' and I won't follow up"). Honor opt-outs right away and mark them in the tracker.
- Only business addresses the prospect publishes. No purchased lists without the owner's approval.

## Approval
Claude creates drafts only. The owner reviews and sends, or tells Claude in the session to send a specific batch. Never send without that instruction.

## Reactivation and referrals (fastest source of work)
Past clients and the contractors behind the 2,145 towers already trust the owner's work. Email each past contact: what you're doing now, ask if anything is coming up, and ask who else they know who needs drone work. Ask happy past clients for a Google review with the direct review link.
