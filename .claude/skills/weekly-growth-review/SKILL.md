# Weekly growth review

1. **Pipeline**: Open the "California Drones Lead Tracker" sheet in Drive. Count rows by status (New, Contacted, Replied, Call booked, Quoted, Won, Lost, Not now, Opted out). List the follow-ups due this week and any quotes older than 5 days.
2. **Outreach**: Draft the follow-ups that are due (cold-outreach skill). Draft first emails for the next 15 to 30 A-priority prospects. Drafts only.
3. **Top up**: If fewer than 40 untouched A or B prospects remain, run the prospector for the weakest segment.
4. **Ads** (if live): Ask the owner for the search-terms and campaign export, then run the google-ads-campaign weekly routine.
5. **Local**: Check the GBP post for this week, new reviews to reply to, and photos to add.
6. **Site**: Note which landing pages produced form leads (the `landing` field in lead emails). Fix the weakest page.
7. **Report**: Give the owner 5 lines: leads this week by source, quotes out, jobs won and revenue, money spent, and the three actions for next week.
