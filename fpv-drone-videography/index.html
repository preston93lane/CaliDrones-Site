An FPV (first-person view) drone is flown by a pilot wearing goggles, so it can move like a camera on a wire: through doors, along a bar, over a pool, down a slide. A one-take fly-through shows a whole place in a single continuous shot, which is why these videos stop people scrolling.

For wide, smooth establishing shots we fly stabilized camera drones in 4K. Most venue projects use both: the FPV run for energy, the aerials for scale.

Planned for safety

Every run is planned and rehearsed. We brief your staff, fly when the space is clear or staged with people who have agreed to be filmed, and use a visual observer outdoors as FAA rules require.

Questions

What is an FPV fly-through?

A continuous video, usually 30 to 90 seconds, flown with a small first-person-view drone that moves through a space in one take, indoors and out.

Is it safe to fly FPV indoors?

Yes, with planning. We walk the route, brief staff, fly when the area is clear or staged with people who've agreed to be filmed, and rehearse before the final takes.

Do you need a permit to film in Los Angeles?

Commercial filming on public property in the City of Los Angeles and much of LA County is permitted through FilmLA. Private property needs the owner's permission. We can fly under your production's permit and supply our certificate and insurance documents.

Can you shoot at events?

Yes, with planning around FAA rules on flying over people and any event restrictions. Stadium events carry temporary flight restrictions, so those shoots need coordination.

Do you edit the video?

Yes. Color-graded footage comes standard, and edited reels and vertical cuts for social are available.

Where do you shoot?

Across Los Angeles, Ventura County and California, with travel nationwide on request.
