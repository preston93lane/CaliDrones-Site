What every visit includes

The same angles, every visit. Progress is obvious when the frame doesn't move.

  • Fixed-angle progress stills
  • An orthomosaic of the site
  • A short progress video
  • Dated, labeled files
  • Optional 3D model
  • Marketing aerials on request

On the first visit we set the vantage points: positions, heights and headings that show the whole job. Every later flight repeats them, so a month's work lines up frame to frame. An orthomosaic each visit gives you a measurable top-down record of the site.

Files are dated and organized the same way every time, ready to drop into your project records or share with owners, lenders and inspectors.

Questions

How often should a site be flown?

Monthly suits most buildings. Fast-moving phases such as grading or framing may need biweekly flights, and rebuilds often use milestone flights at clearing, foundation, framing, dry-in and completion.

Do you fly over workers?

We plan flights to follow FAA rules on operations over people and the site's own safety rules, and we coordinate with the superintendent on where and when to fly.

Can the files go into our project software?

Yes. Stills, video and orthomosaics are delivered in standard formats you can upload to your project management or document system.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes, naming your company or the owner as additional insured when your contract requires it.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Single flights are available. A recurring schedule keeps the angles consistent and is usually the better value; it's priced in your quote.

Do you work outside Los Angeles?

Yes. We fly construction sites across Los Angeles and Ventura County and throughout California.
