Privacy

When you send a project request, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, phone number, company, project location and project description. We use them only to reply to you and to plan and quote your project.

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through Web3Forms, a form delivery service. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and we don't use advertising trackers on this site.

To ask what we have on file or to have it deleted, email hello@californiadrones.io.
