## 2. Story and positioning

**What we do:** We put a certified pilot and the right aircraft over sites that are hard, slow or dangerous to see from the ground, and hand back files people can use.

**Why the brand looks the way it does.** Our work has two sides, and the identity holds both:

- **Golden hour** is the cinematic side: real estate at dusk, FPV runs through a waterpark, the California light everyone recognizes. It's the gold sun in the mark and every call to action.
- **Scan** is the technical side: 2,145 towers documented sector by sector, point clouds, orthomosaics, airspace authorizations. It's the cyan data accent, the point cloud on the website and the precise, wide type.

Clients in telecom and construction need to trust that we're rigorous. Clients in real estate and hospitality need to see that we have an eye. **We lead with rigor and show the eye in the results.**

**Positioning statement.** For telecom contractors, builders, property marketers and brands across California who need aerial work done right the first time, California Drones is the FAA Part 107 certified, fully insured operator that brings inspection-grade discipline (2,145 cell towers, 26 states and territories) to every flight, from a sector photo set to a golden-hour FPV run.

**Audiences, and what each one needs to hear**

| Audience | They care about | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Telecom contractors, tower engineers, tower owners | Complete, standard-compliant data; no re-flights; safety; vendor paperwork | 2,145 towers on AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon sites; organized, labeled files |
| GCs, developers, civil and survey firms | Reliable cadence, measurable outputs, airspace handled | Orthomosaics, 3D models, volumes; monthly progress flights; LAANC |
| Listing agents and photographers | Fast turnaround, images that sell, compliance | Twilight and vertical cuts; Part 107 and insured; LAANC near LAX, BUR and VNY |
| Venues, hospitality, brands, producers | Scroll-stopping footage, safe indoor flights, a pro on set | Raging Waters FPV; color-graded 4K; edited reels |

## 3. Personality and voice

**Personality:** precise, calm, capable, warm, straight-talking. Like the pilot you want on a busy job site: unflappable, prepared, easy to deal with.

**We are not:** hype-driven, gimmicky, "drone bro," corporate-jargony, or cute.

### Voice rules
1. **Specific over impressive.** "Sector-by-sector photo sets" beats "comprehensive solutions."
2. **Numbers over adjectives.** "2,145 towers" does the work "extensive experience" can't.
3. **Short, active sentences.** Say who does what. "Airspace is checked and authorized."
4. **Talk about their outcome.** "Your engineers can find problems from the ground."
5. **Technical when the reader is technical.** Say "plumb and twist," "orthomosaic," "LAANC" to people who use those words. Explain them for homeowners.
6. **Calm confidence.** No exclamation marks, no superlatives we can't prove.

### Words
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| inspect, capture, document, map, measure, fly, deliver | stunning, epic, breathtaking, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, game-changing, unparalleled |
| site, sector, flight, files, model, footage, quote | solutions, synergy, leverage, bird's-eye view, "drone guy" |
| certified, insured, authorized | "licensed drone pilot" (say "FAA Part 107 certified") |
| drone (to the public); UAS (in government or telecom paperwork) | UAV in marketing copy |

### Before and after
- ❌ "We offer cutting-edge aerial solutions for all your needs!"
  ✅ "Drone inspections, 3D models and aerial film, flown across California."
- ❌ "Breathtaking drone photography that will wow buyers."
  ✅ "Aerial stills and twilight video that show the lot, the view and the approach."
- ❌ "Our experienced team provides comprehensive tower inspection services."
  ✅ "2,145 towers inspected. Antenna and mount close-ups your engineers can work from."

### Mechanics
- **Headings** in sentence case: "Request a quote," not "Request A Quote."
- **Numbers** as numerals: 2,145; 8+ years; 26 states and territories; 4K.
- **Phone:** (805) 746-9775. **Email:** hello@californiadrones.io (lowercase).
- **Credentials**, always written this way: "FAA Part 107 certified," "Fully insured."
- **CTAs:** "Request a quote" (primary), "Call (805) 746-9775" (secondary), "Quote a [service]" (in context). Never "Submit" or "Click here."
- **Oxford comma** off ("photo, video and 3D models"), as on the site.

## 5. Logo

### Anatomy
The **Golden hour mark** is a gold sun (`#F2C230`) cut by **four horizon bands** that get thicker toward the ground, with a camera drone silhouetted in front. It reads as a drone at golden hour, and the bands double as scan lines.
The **wordmark** is CALIFORNIA DRONES in Big Shoulders Display ExtraBold, uppercase, +0.02em tracking. In the files it is converted to outlines, so it never depends on installed fonts.

### Files (`brand/logo/`)
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `lockup-horizontal-on-dark.svg` | **Primary logo.** Website header, email signature, dark documents |
| `lockup-horizontal-on-light.svg` | Primary logo on paper or light photos |
| `lockup-stacked-on-dark.svg` / `-on-light.svg` | Square-ish spaces: decals, apparel, title slides |
| `mark.svg` | Mark alone on any background (the bands are cut out, so the background shows through) |
| `mark-app.svg` | App icon / favicon: mark on an Abyss rounded square |
| `mark-mono-dark.svg` / `mark-mono-light.svg` | One color only: vinyl decals, embroidery, stamps, engraving |
| `wordmark-on-dark.svg` / `-on-light.svg` | Wordmark alone, when the mark appears nearby |
| `png/` | Ready-made PNGs: profile pictures, Google Business Profile logo, social banner, link preview |

### Clear space and size
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the horizon bands block (about ⅓ of the mark's height) on every side. Nothing else enters it.
- **Minimum size:** mark 16px or 5mm; horizontal lockup 120px or 30mm wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

### Backgrounds
- Best: Abyss or any dark, calm photo area (sky, shadow).
- On paper or white: use the `on-light` lockups. The gold sun still works; the drone and wordmark go Abyss.
- On busy photos: place it on a solid Abyss bar or in a darkened corner. Never on gold.

### Don't
Recolor the sun · Change the number or order of bands · Rotate or tilt · Stretch · Add shadows, glows, gradients or outlines · Set the wordmark in another font · Put the full-color mark on a gold or yellow background · Rebuild the drone from clip art.

## 7. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark, uppercase labels, poster headlines | **Big Shoulders Display** ExtraBold 800 | Uppercase only, +0.02em tracking. Never for sentences |
| Headlines | **Archivo** variable, weight 740, width 116–120 | Tight leading (1.0–1.06), −0.015 to −0.022em tracking, sentence case |
| Body | Archivo 400, width 100 | 17px / 1.6 on screen; 10–11pt / 1.45 in print |
| Labels, meta | Archivo 520–600 | 14px |
| Buttons | Archivo 650, width 108 | |
| Big numbers | Archivo **320, width 125**, tabular figures | Huge and light: the signature "data" look (2,145) |

**Why these:** Archivo was designed for both screens and print and has a width axis. Widened, it feels engineered and confident, like instrument labels; at normal width it's an easy read. Big Shoulders Display is a condensed, Chicago-born display face that's tall, industrial and uppercase, so the wordmark reads like a stencil on a flight case. Both are free under the SIL Open Font License from Google Fonts.

**Scale (screen):** 14 · 17 · 20 · 30→48 (H2) · 37→56 (H1) · 54→112 (stats). Fluid values are in `tokens.css`.

**In Google Docs, Word and Slides:** use **Archivo** (or **Archivo SemiExpanded/Expanded** for headings if available) and **Big Shoulders Display** for the cover title. Install them on a computer from fonts.google.com. Fallback if a font is missing: Arial / Helvetica.

**Rules:** Two typefaces, no third. Don't use all caps in Archivo body text. Don't use faux bold or italics for emphasis in headlines; let weight and width do it.

## 8. Layout and components

- **Grid:** 1240px max width, fluid gutter `clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px)`. Two-column splits run 5:7. Text measure ≤ 34rem.
- **Spacing:** 4px base; section padding 72–128px.
- **Shape:** 8px radius on controls, 14px on panels, pill chips. Hairline 1px rules instead of boxes wherever possible.
- **Buttons:** *Signal*: Golden Hour fill with on-signal text, the one primary action per view. *Line*: 1.5px `#2C6377` border, Scan border on hover. Min height 50px (42px small).
- **Chips:** pill, Field fill, Field-line border; selected = Golden Hour.
- **Forms:** Panel card; inputs on Field with Field-line border; focus = Scan border + 3px Scan glow at 20%. Errors in Coral.
- **Focus ring:** 3px Golden Hour outline, 3px offset. Never remove it.

## 9. Graphic devices

Use one or two per piece, not all of them.

1. **Horizon bands.** The four bands from the mark (thin to thick, top to bottom, gaps roughly equal to the bands) as a footer stripe, a divider or the base of a social post. Abyss on gold or gold on Abyss.
2. **Scan diamonds.** 7px squares rotated 45° in Scan, used as bullets for deliverables.
3. **Gold rule.** A 2px Golden Hour line on the left of a key figure ("**2,145** towers inspected…").
4. **Big light numerals.** Archivo 320 at width 125, very large, with a Mist caption underneath. For stats only.
5. **Flight path.** A dashed line (6px dash, 7px gap) in `#2C6377` connecting steps.
6. **Point cloud.** Fine dots in Scan at 10–40% opacity, as on the website's 3D stage. For backgrounds behind mapping and inspection content.
7. **HUD frame.** Corner brackets plus a small coordinate or altitude label in Mist (e.g. `34.05° N 118.24° W · 120 FT AGL`). For inspection and mapping imagery only, at most once per piece, and only with real data from that flight.

## 10. Photography and video

**Overall:** real work, real sites, true color. The sun is our logo, so golden hour and blue hour are our signature light whenever the job allows.

| Service | Look |
|---|---|
| Cell towers | Documentary and clinical. Even light, sharp detail, level frames, consistent sector order. Show the deliverable (close-ups, orbit stills), not "hero" glamour. |
| Mapping and 3D | Top-down orthos and oblique views of models; overlay measurements in Scan. Show scale: trucks, people, equipment. |
| Construction | The same vantage points every month so progress is obvious. Clean, high-contrast, mid-morning or golden hour. |
| Real estate | Golden hour and twilight. Level horizons, natural color, no fisheye, no oversaturated skies. Lot lines in Golden Hour only if the client approves. |
| FPV and film | Smooth, continuous, purposeful paths. Motion that reveals the space. Never shaky or disorienting for its own sake. |

**Always:** level horizons; crisp focus; honest color (no fake sky swaps on real estate without disclosure).
**Never show:** flights over crowds or moving traffic, unauthorized airspace, people without consent, or anything a client hasn't cleared for public use. Our compliance is part of the brand.
**Watermarks:** none on client deliverables. On our own social posts, the small mono mark bottom-right at 40% opacity is optional.
**Captions:** place · service · what the client got. "Simi Valley · Monthly progress flight · Orthomosaic + 40 stills."

## 11. Motion

Move like a stabilized gimbal: smooth, eased, deliberate. Default ease `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`; UI changes take 160–250ms; reveals take about 1.3s. No bounce, no spin, no shake. Numbers can count up once. Always respect *reduce motion*: show the final state.

## 12. Icons

Simple line icons with a 1.75px stroke, round caps and joins, on a 24px grid, in Mist or Scan (Golden Hour only when the icon is the action). [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC license) matches well. No filled clip-art, emoji or 3D icons in brand materials.

## 13. Applications

- **Website:** `/styles.css` implements these tokens. New pages follow `.claude/skills/service-landing-page/SKILL.md`.
- **Email signature:** `templates/email-signature.html` (HTML plus a plain-text version inside).
- **Business card:** `templates/business-card.html`, printed to PDF at 3.75 × 2.25 in (includes 0.125 in bleed). Front: Abyss, mark, horizon bands. Back: name, title, phone, email, URL, "FAA Part 107 certified · Fully insured."
- **Social profiles:** profile picture `logo/png/avatar-1080.png` (mark on Abyss, safe inside a circle crop). Banner `logo/png/banner-linkedin-1584x396.png`.
- **Link previews:** `/img/og-image.png` (1200×630) is set as the site's share image. `/img/email-logo.png` is the hosted logo for the email signature.
- **Google Business Profile:** logo `logo/png/gbp-logo-720.png`. Cover: a real golden-hour job photo, not a graphic.
- **Social posts:** 4:5 (1080×1350) for feed, 9:16 for reels and stories. Real footage first; brand bar (Abyss with horizon bands and the mono mark) at most at the bottom 8%. One line of text, Archivo Bold wide, Haze. CTA in captions: "Request a quote at californiadrones.io."
- **Ads:** follow the voice rules. Lead with a proof point (2,145 towers; Part 107 and insured; Raging Waters FPV). No prices unless they're published on the site.
- **Proposals, quotes, invoices:** Paper background, horizontal lockup top-left, Archivo throughout, Amber ink rules, totals in Archivo 320 wide. Number quotes `CD-Q-YYYY-###`.
- **Deliverables:** organized and labeled, as the website promises.
  Folder: `CD_<Client>_<Site or Project>_<YYYY-MM-DD>/`
  Files: `<SiteID>_<Sector or Area>_<Type>_<###>.jpg` (e.g. `LA1234_SectorA_AntennaCloseup_007.jpg`). Include a short `README.txt` with the date, pilot, aircraft, airspace authorization and the contents.
- **Field kit:** a hi-vis vest with the mono-light mark on the back, mono decals on flight cases (`mark-mono-light.svg`), a vehicle magnet with the stacked lockup on Abyss. Looking professional on site is marketing.

## 14. Keeping it consistent

- The tokens are the source of truth. Change `brand/tokens.css`, then mirror the change in `tokens.json` and the `:root` block of `/styles.css`.
- Logo SVGs are generated with outlined text. If the wordmark ever changes, regenerate all lockups together.
- Claims in any brand material come only from `.claude/business-facts.md`.
- The `brand/` folder is excluded from the deployed site (see `.assetsignore`), except where files are copied into the site on purpose (the favicon and the files in `/img/`).
