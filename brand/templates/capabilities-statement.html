Capabilities statement

Flown right.

California Drones is an FAA Part 107 certified, fully insured drone services company based in Los Angeles. It serves all of California and travels nationwide on request. We capture inspection imagery, maps, 3D models and aerial film, and deliver them as organized, labeled files your team can use right away.

Core capabilities

Cell tower inspectionsSector-by-sector photo sets, antenna and mount close-ups, orbit and plumb video, closeout and condition documentation.

Mapping, 3D models and photogrammetryOrthomosaic maps, point clouds, textured 3D models, measurements and stockpile volumes; flown on your surveyor's ground control when survey-grade accuracy is needed.

Construction progress flightsMonthly or milestone flights from fixed vantage points, with an orthomosaic, stills and video each visit.

Real estate photo and videoEdited aerial stills, listing and walkthrough video, twilight shoots, vertical cuts for social.

Cinematic aerial and FPV4K stabilized aerials, one-take FPV fly-throughs, color-graded footage and edited reels for venues, events and brands.
